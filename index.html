Primary Training Goals

As a leader your job is to
  • Get your EL comfortable, confident, and excited immediately
  • Stay with them at all times — Holster Theory
  • Explain everything happening at all times
  • Talk big game and vision cast the opportunity
  • Build trust by leading by example first
  • Promote your EL in every leaders meeting
  • Be first to greet them every single day
  • Walk them to their car at end of every day
The process model
  • I do → You watch me succeed first
  • We do → We work side by side
  • You do → They execute independently
  • Get sales on board before Days 1–3
  • BCB every day — Build, Coach, Build
  • 3 strengths for every 1 weakness
  • Only 1–2 coaching points per day
  • Always say “we” not “I”

Sales Training & Success in Sales

The skill progression
  • BEAST factors — foundation of every interaction
  • Qualifying questions to find the right customer
  • CPR — build a connection within 30 seconds
  • FUJI factors to personalize every pitch
  • Height of impulse — close at the peak
  • Objection Triangle — Relate, Impulse, Reclose
  • Power of Ten — one thing for 10 pitches in a row
  • 80/20 Rule and the 4 KRAs

The Mentality of Training

How great leaders think
  • Everything is caught, not taught — model it first
  • Give all credit to the system, never yourself
  • 50% good rule, 200% bad rule
  • Ask: personal trainer or cheerleader?
  • Tell them when right AND when wrong
  • Never criticize a person — only address actions
  • Always leave them on a good note

Main focus: Make it fun and easy — make them feel like anyone can do it
Before leaving the office
  • Be prepared: Clean car, gas up, all supplies ready
  • Promote EL in leaders meeting — school, hometown, last job, sports
  • Be first to greet your EL — keep them at your side all day
  • Give brief office tour — bags, rooms, morning spot, manager office
  • Introduce to top performers in the office
  • Explain practice pitch, games, 4s/5s/8s, entry-level expectations
  • Have them write down the pitch — focus on intro and short story
  • Let them know they will NOT have to speak today
In the car / setting expectations
  • Get out with urgency — first one out is first to make money
  • Go over goals, intro, and short story on the ride
  • Ask: “Personal trainer or cheerleader?” Explain the difference
  • Tell them you’ll say when right AND when wrong
In the field
  • Give 3 options: watch me / one-and-one / jump right in
  • B.E.A.S.T. factors and why they matter
  • Qualifying questions — only customers paying $150+
  • Intro / short story — get these down solid
  • LOAs — talk to 80–100 people
  • Turn and burn back to leader
  • First half of the presentation — find hot buttons
  • After every sale: explain what worked — say “we” not “I”
  • EL does 100% of iPad work
  • Take 10–15 min breaks to bond if needed
  • Get a sale — NOT more than 2 above entry level
End of day
  • Review LOAS — what went well, what to improve, gameplan tomorrow
  • Walk EL to their car — boost confidence, leave on a good note
  • Ask if they have questions — tell them they were awesome
